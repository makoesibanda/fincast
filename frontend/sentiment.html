What is this page?

This page fetches live financial news headlines from CoinDesk, CryptoPanic and BBC Business, then scores each one using FinBERT - a language model trained specifically on financial text.

The sentiment score shown here reflects what the news is saying, not the current BTC price. A positive score means headlines are leaning bullish today. A negative score means the mood in the news is bearish.

This score is also fed directly into the forecasting model as one of its 19 input features.
